# ClaimRush Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

## Why a CLA?

ClaimRush is released under the MIT License. This CLA ensures the project
maintains the ability to manage its licensing in the future — for example,
adding a patent-grant license or dual-licensing — without needing to track
down every past contributor for permission.

Signing this CLA does **not** transfer ownership of your work. You retain
full copyright over your contributions.
